    Grab chapters from a website

    Chrome on Android has no extensions, but a bookmarklet does the same job: on a chapter page of any novel site (webnovel.com, novelping and others) it grabs that chapter, follows the next-chapter links to collect the following ones and sends them here.

    1. Tap Copy short bookmarklet.
    2. In Chrome, bookmark any page (menu, then the star). Then menu, Bookmarks, long-press it, Edit.
    3. Name it reader, replace the URL with the copied text, save.
    4. On a chapter page, tap the address bar, type reader and tap the bookmark result (the one with the star, not a search).

      Listen with the screen off

      0:00

      Chrome pauses the voice engine when the app is in the background or the screen is off. Save chapters first: the voice is generated now, while the screen is on, and kept on your phone (about 10 MB per hour). Playback in the background then just plays the saved files.

      Keep this app open with the screen on while it works; it keeps the screen awake for you, and you can listen at the same time. Saved audio belongs to the current voice, so changing voice means saving again. Chapters you finished are cleaned up automatically.

      Voice and playback

      Voice: Kokoro-82M, open source and running on your device. The first launch downloads about 330 MB (use Wi-Fi); after that it works offline. Lock-screen and headphone buttons skip back or forward one passage.

      While you listen with the screen on, the app keeps the chosen amount saved ahead, so you can turn the screen off at any moment. When it gets close to running out, the engine works at full speed whatever the GPU setting.
