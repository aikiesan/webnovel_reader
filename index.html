    📲 Grab chapters from a website (bookmarklet)

    Chrome on Android has no extensions, but a bookmarklet does the same job: on a chapter page of any novel site (webnovel.com, novelping, etc.) it grabs that chapter and follows the "Next" links to collect the following ones and sends them here.

    1. Tap Copy SHORT bookmarklet.
    2. In Chrome, bookmark any page (⋮ → ☆). Then ⋮ → Bookmarks → long-press it → Edit.
    3. Name it reader, replace the URL with the copied text, save.
    4. On a webnovel chapter, tap the address bar, type reader and tap the bookmark result (the one with the ☆, not a search).

      Listen with the screen off

      Chrome pauses the voice engine when the app is in the background or the screen is off. To keep listening, prepare chapters first: the voice is generated now, while the screen is on, and saved on your phone (about 10 MB per hour). After that, playback in the background just plays the saved files.

      Keep this app open with the screen on while it prepares (it keeps the screen awake for you). You can keep listening at the same time. Saved audio is for the current voice; changing voice means preparing again. Finished chapters are cleaned up automatically.

      Voice & playback

      Voice: Kokoro-82M, open-source and running on your device. The first launch downloads ~330 MB (use Wi‑Fi); after that it works offline. Lock-screen and headphone buttons skip back or forward one passage.
